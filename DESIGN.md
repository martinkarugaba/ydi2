## Brand & Style

This design system expresses hope, community empowerment, and structural transparency for youth and grassroots initiatives. Drawing directly from the vitality of community-driven development in Uganda, the visual personality balances institutional integrity with grassroots warmth. It evokes optimism, trust, collective action, and humane care.

The design movement is **Clean Modern Humanist**: an aesthetic grounded in generous negative space, crisp and airy surfaces, gentle ambient light, and distinct multi-accent color coding. Rather than leaning into heavy, saturated backdrops or muddy earth tones, interfaces remain featherweight, open, and vibrant. Information architecture emphasizes clarity, legible typographic hierarchy, and approachable touch targets that make programmatic impacts immediately understandable to donors, volunteers, and local partners.

## Colors

The palette directly honors the logo's cobalt-to-coral progression while avoiding heavy, reddish-brown, muddy peach, or crimson-dominant surfaces. Instead, surfaces are pristine, cool, and breathable, enriched with intentional accents:

- **Primary (`#1D4ED8`):** Royal Cobalt Blue anchored at the core of the brand. Represents institutional solidity, dignity, and primary interactive hierarchy.
- **Secondary (`#D97706`):** Warm Golden Amber / Marigold. Expresses community warmth, optimism, education, and shared celebration. Used for key callouts, engagement metrics, and secondary actions.
- **Tertiary (`#059669`):** Grassroots Emerald / Teal. Reflects sustainable agricultural programs, youth growth, environmental stewardship, and positive impact badges.
- **Humanitarian Accent (`#E11D48`):** Soft Rose Coral. Derived from the upper gradient of the hands in the logo. Used deliberately and sparingly for donation drives, health interventions, and heart-centered alerts to prevent visual fatigue.
- **Surfaces & Neutrals:** Backgrounds avoid warm muddiness. They consist of pure white (`#FFFFFF`), light ice-slate (`#F8FAFC`), and subtle morning sky tint (`#F0F6FE`). Body text is set in deep slate (`#0F172A`), providing AAA-compliant legibility across all viewport conditions.

## Typography

**Plus Jakarta Sans** is employed across all hierarchy tiers. Its friendly apertures, distinct geometric curves, and open counters provide approachable readability across both desktop platforms and low-bandwidth mobile devices.

- **Display & Headlines:** Set with tight tracking and medium-to-bold weights (600–800) to project confidence, community strength, and forward movement without appearing harsh.
- **Body:** Uses regular weights (400) with generous line heights (1.5–1.55x) to guarantee effortless scanning of field updates, volunteer reports, and project narratives.
- **Labels & Microcopy:** Employs medium-to-semibold weights (600) with slightly expanded letter tracking for tabular figures, category badges, and metric cards.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an adaptive 12-column responsive grid on desktop and tablet, collapsing gracefully to a 4-column layout on mobile devices. 

- **Desktop (>= 1024px):** Max container width of 1240px with 2rem outer margins and 1.5rem gutters. Sections embrace substantial vertical rhythm (4rem to 6rem vertical gaps) to maintain an uplifting, open atmosphere.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8 columns, 1.5rem outer margins, 1.25rem gutters. Split views reconfigure into modular vertical stacks.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, 1.25rem outer margins, 1rem gutters. Interactive cards expand across full row widths, preserving minimum touch targets of 48px.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through clean, tonal surface layerings combined with delicate, cool-tinted ambient shadows, rejecting dirty or heavy drop shadows:

- **Surface Layering:** Base canvases use `#F8FAFC` (Ice Slate) or `#FFFFFF`. Card containers sit directly on top with `#FFFFFF`, creating crisp contrast. Accent panels leverage ultra-soft tints such as `#EFF6FF` (5% Cobalt tint) or `#F0FDF4` (5% Emerald tint).
- **Ambient Elevation:** Shadows are soft, wide, and tinted with deep slate (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05)`). Raised elements use an offset of `0px 4px 20px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06)`. Hover states gracefully elevate with `0px 12px 28px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.09)`.
- **Structural Outlines:** For clean organization, cards and inputs feature low-contrast hairline borders (`1px solid #E2E8F0`). Under active focus, borders transition to `#1D4ED8` paired with an accessible 3px outer glow (`rgba(29, 78, 216, 0.15)`).

## Shapes

The design system standardizes on a roundedness level of `2` (8px standard corner radius):

- **Standard Components (Buttons, Form Controls, Inputs):** 8px (`rounded-md`), establishing an approachable, trustworthy, and modern feel that avoids severe geometric sharpness.
- **Cards & Modals:** 16px (`rounded-lg`), delivering a softer framing that complements the organic shapes and silhouettes in the logo.
- **Badges, Tags & Avatars:** Fully rounded pill shapes (`rounded-full` / 9999px) to echo the circular and flowing heart-and-hand logo contours.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background `#1D4ED8`, text `#FFFFFF`, 8px border-radius, font-weight 600. Hover shifts to `#1E40AF`.
- **Secondary (Community/Action):** Background `#D97706`, text `#FFFFFF`, 8px border-radius. Hover transitions to `#B45309`.
- **Tertiary (Grassroots/Growth):** Background `#059669`, text `#FFFFFF`, 8px border-radius. Hover transitions to `#047857`.
- **Humanitarian/Donate (Accent):** Background `#E11D48`, text `#FFFFFF`, 8px border-radius. Reserved strictly for contribution and emergency appeals.
- **Outline / Ghost:** Border `1.5px solid #CBD5E1`, text `#0F172A`, transparent background. On hover, fills with light sky tint `#F0F6FE`.

### Badges & Chips
- **Status & Focus Badges:** Full-pill radius (`rounded-full`), padding `4px 12px`, font-size 12px semibold. 
- **Palette Pairing:** 
  - Education/Youth: `#EFF6FF` background with `#1D4ED8` text.
  - Community/Empowerment: `#FEF3C7` background with `#92400E` text.
  - Agriculture/Eco: `#ECFDF5` background with `#065F46` text.
  - Relief/Health: `#FFE4E6` background with `#9F1239` text.

### Form Inputs & Controls
- **Text Inputs:** Height 44px, padding `0 14px`, 8px border-radius, border `1px solid #CBD5E1`, background `#FFFFFF`. Active state transitions border to `#1D4ED8` with a 3px soft focus halo (`rgba(29, 78, 216, 0.15)`).
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** 20px size, 4px border-radius for checkboxes, circular for radios. Checked fill uses `#1D4ED8` with pure white indicator icons.

### Cards & Content Containers
- **Standard Card:** Crisp white surface (`#FFFFFF`), border `1px solid #F1F5F9`, 16px border-radius, subtle ambient shadow. Padding is scaled dynamically (`1.25rem` mobile, `1.75rem` desktop).
- **Feature/Initiative Card:** Highlights key impact metrics using tinted top border accents (3px stripe of `#1D4ED8`, `#D97706`, or `#059669`) to immediately differentiate program areas.

### Lists & Activity Feeds
- **Feed Items:** Separated by thin hairline dividers (`#F1F5F9`), utilizing 40px circular avatar containers featuring soft pastel thematic fills and crisp icons.
